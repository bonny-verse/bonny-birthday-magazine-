About the Magazine

The Birthday Edition

A digital birthday magazine celebrating Bonny. Laid out like a printed issue, it brings together different sides of her life — her childhood, her path into architecture, her drawing, her travels, her love of cats and her personal style — as a keepsake of who she is and the journey so far.

How to Use the Magazine

Everything you can do
Turn the pages
Swipe or dragDrag a page sideways to peel it, then let go: it either settles back or flips over. Swipe left for the next page, right to go back.
TapTap the right half of the magazine to go forward and the left half to go back. A soft paper sound plays on every turn.
Prev & NextThe two buttons at the bottom turn pages too, and the counter between them shows the pages you are on (Cover, 2 – 3 / 16 … Back cover).
Move the 3D magazine
Rotate the viewOn a phone or tablet, tap the cube button to turn 3D mode on, then drag anywhere to look at the cover, spine and pages from any angle. Flick and it glides on by itself. Page turning is paused while 3D is on. Tap the cube again and the magazine glides back to the reading view.
On a computerThere is no cube button: press and drag on the empty space around the magazine to rotate it, and drag on the magazine itself to turn pages.
Reset the viewTap the reset icon, or tap the “Bonny · The Birthday Edition” tag in the top-left corner, to bring the magazine back to its starting position, zoom and first page.
Zoom & pan
Zoom in and outPinch with two fingers, double-tap (double-click), use the magnifier buttons, or scroll the mouse wheel. You can zoom up to 4×.
Look aroundWhile zoomed, drag to move around the page. Push past the edge to turn the page, and double-tap again to return to the full view.
Read page by page
PDF modeTap the document icon to read in a flat, full-screen viewer with no 3D. Choose Slide to swipe one page at a time, or Scroll to read straight down like a long page.
Zoom inside PDF modeUse the − and + buttons, pinch, or double-tap to zoom, and drag to move around. Tap × (or press Esc) to go back to the 3D magazine.
Music & sound
Reading musicTap the music-note button to open the music panel. Pick a calm track: Soft Piano, Music Box Lullaby, Warm Evening Glow, Rainy Window or Lo-fi Library. Tap it again to stop. Music pauses by itself when you leave the page.
VolumeSlide the volume control in the music panel to make the music softer or louder. Tap × or anywhere outside to close the panel.
Screen & buttons
FullscreenTap the corner-arrows icon to enter or leave fullscreen. iPhone Safari has no fullscreen: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and open the magazine from there.
Rotate screenOn a phone or tablet, tap the rotate button to switch between landscape and portrait. If your phone cannot do it, turn it by hand with auto-rotate on.
More buttonsIn portrait on a phone, the Reset, Info and PDF buttons are tucked behind the “…” button. Tap it to show them.
Hide the buttonsTap the eye icon for a clean, distraction-free view with every button hidden. Tap the eye again to bring them back.
This guideTap the “i” button for About the Magazine, About Bonny, and this guide.
Keyboard shortcuts
On a computer← → or PageUp PageDown turn pages · + − zoom · 0 resets the zoom · Esc closes this card or PDF mode.
